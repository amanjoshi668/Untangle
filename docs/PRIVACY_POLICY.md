# Untangle — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 21, 2026

Untangle ("Strategic Decision Studio") is offline-first. You can use every technique without an account.

1. **Guest mode.** Decisions, notes, bookmarks and reminder preferences are stored only on your device.
2. **Optional account.** If you register (email/password or Google), we store your name, email, a hashed password (scrypt) or Google account ID, and a snapshot of your decision vault, solely to sync across your devices.
3. **Technique suggestions.** Short descriptions are matched on-device. When online and you type a longer description (10+ words), the text may be sent statelessly to our server and Google's Gemini API to suggest techniques. It is not used for advertising or sold.
4. **Notifications.** Optional reminders are scheduled locally by Android; no notification data leaves the device.
5. **Bug reports.** If you submit one, we store the description, the screen name, app version, and your email (if signed in) to resolve it.
6. **Export & deletion.** Export your data as `.md` or `.json` from Settings & Vault, delete decisions any time, or request account deletion via the support contact on the store listing.
7. **Children.** Untangle is not directed at children under 13.
