# Untangle — Play Store Listing

**Title (≤30):** Untangle: Decision Models

**Short description (≤80):** Structure decisions with 52 interactive mental models, matcher, and journal.

**Full description:**
Untangle is an offline-first decision-making and strategic thinking workspace that organizes 52 well-known decision models into practical, interactive tools. Whether you are prioritizing daily tasks, weighing two job offers, evaluating a project, or facilitating a team discussion, Untangle helps you pick a structured framework and work through it step by step.

TWO WAYS TO START ANY DECISION
• Decide (Problem-to-Model Matcher): Type your dilemma, goal, or challenge in plain language and filter by context (Any context, Personal, Team & Others, or Quick ≤2 min). Untangle recommends the best-fitting model along with two alternative perspectives and pre-fills your decision topic into the workspace so you do not have to type it twice.
• Techniques (Direct 52-Model Library & Book Compass): Search all 52 models by name, keyword, or category across four sections—Improve Yourself, Understand Yourself, Understand Others, and Improve Others—or switch to the interactive Me/Others × Doing/Thinking Compass to browse visually.

INTERACTIVE DECISION WORKSPACES (8 TOOL TYPES)
• 2×2 Matrices: Sort tasks and factors into quadrants with the Eisenhower Matrix, SWOT Analysis, BCG Box, Project Portfolio Matrix, Johari Window, Feedback Model, and Appreciative Inquiry.
• Weighted Force Balances: Weigh opposing factors from 1 to 5 in the Rubber Band Model, Cognitive Dissonance Model, Pareto 80/20 Principle, and Prisoner's Dilemma.
• Live Charts & Sliders: Adjust Challenge vs. Skill in the Flow Model, score Have To / Able To / Want To in the Personal Performance Sail, allocate Past/Present/Future time in the Energy Model, and map team skills or market gaps.
• Checklists & Stage Steppers: Validate goals with the John Whitmore SMART/PURE/CLEAR checklist, audit mistakes with the Swiss Cheese Model, and diagnose team or leadership stages with Drexler–Sibbet and Hersey–Blanchard.
• Creative & Planning Tools: Combine options in the Morphological Box, explore perspectives with De Bono's Six Thinking Hats, split deadlines into three loops with the Result Optimisation Model, compare six paths in the Crossroads Model, or run a 1,000-trial range simulation with Monte Carlo.

BUILT-IN TECHNIQUE GUIDES & DECISION JOURNAL
• Tap the "?" button on any recommendation, library row, or workspace to open a compact summary explaining how the technique works, how to apply it, and related models to try next.
• Save completed workspaces to your Decision Journal with an automatic summary takeaway and SuperMemo spaced review check-ins (Day 1, Day 10, Day 30, and Day 60).
• Export your decisions as Markdown (.md) briefs or JSON backups, set optional daily focus and evening reflection reminders, switch between Light Paper and Dark Charcoal themes, and use the app offline in Guest Mode with optional account sync.
