# Untangle — Play Store Listing

**Title (≤30):** Untangle: Decision Models

**Short description (≤80):** Describe any dilemma and get the right thinking model — 52 tools, offline.

**Full description:**
Stuck between two good options? Drowning in urgent tasks? Team going in circles?

Untangle gives you 52 classic strategic thinking models as calm, interactive tools — and helps you pick the right one.

• Tell it your problem — describe it in plain words and Untangle suggests the best-fit model, then pre-fills it for you.
• Or browse all 52 — search, filter by time, or explore the Me/Others × Doing/Thinking compass.
• Interactive, not textbook — 2×2 matrices, weighted dilemma balances, live charts, checklists, idea combiners, a 1,000-trial Monte Carlo simulator and more.
• Every model explained in a tap — a short plain-English writeup and "how to use" tip behind the ? button.
• A takeaway every time — each tool turns your inputs into a clear next step.
• Journal with spaced reviews — revisit decisions at Day 1, 10, 30 and 60.
• Private by default — works fully offline with no sign-up; optional sync.
• Light and dark themes, phone and tablet layouts.
